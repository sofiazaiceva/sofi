“A journey through computer music marbled with fleeting, iridescent melodies, curious harmonic resonance, and a mix of sublime and fractious impulses... the music treated as living matter, coding serving as a form of live improvisation.”
THE QUIETUS — 2026

Sofi (Sofia Zaiceva) is a Latvia-born sound artist and performer who approaches sound as a process of controlled instability, using code to deconstruct and redirect the digital signal into an aesthetically fluid and compositionally shifting post-club sound. Trained as a classical pianist before moving into electronics, she works with open source software, mainly SuperCollider, writing algorithms that shift and respond as she plays them.

Data hijacking and digital archaeology are central to the practice. She works with obsolete devices, dead operating systems and the residue left on them by whoever used them last, intercepting what they still emit. The devices carry traces of the people who owned them, and they shape the piece as much as she does.

She has played Skaņu Mežs in Riga, ORF musikprotokoll in Graz, ZiMMT in Leipzig, Musiikkitalo in Helsinki, MeetFactory in Prague and Nonagon Festival in Sweden. Recent collaborations include an audiovisual duo with Ľubomír Slovinský and Branches & Bytes with Andreas Trobollowitsch. Her music comes out on Superpang, SØVN Records, Riforma and mappa, and she was selected for the SHAPE+ platform in 2024/25.
